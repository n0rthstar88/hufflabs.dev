About

My brother had just bought a Sony VAIO running Windows 95, a brand-new $3,000 machine, and I needed to know what was inside. I took it apart and got it back together working. He was furious. I was hooked.

That curiosity turned into A+ and Network+ certifications in high school, then a job in Walmart's tech department, working with game systems, TVs, printers, the photo lab and wireless phones, back in the days of 3G and physical SIM cards.

I wanted more. After roles in technical training and federal tax compliance, I joined Vero Networks during the pandemic and moved into a lead role. That work also started my homelab, which grew quickly and led me to learn everything I could about AI.
